Profile Building Guidelines

To build a complete profile that represents your true professional identity on CoreLink, please follow the guidelines below. Each section is designed to help you showcase your actual value to the community, moving beyond a simple list to a true reflection of your capabilities.

Profile Progress {% if portfolio.oracle_score %}{{ portfolio.oracle_score }}{% else %}0{% endif %}%

Profile Image & Cover

Upload a clean professional profile image of you with clear resolution that lets people see who you are. Your profile image is the first point of connection, so choose something that feels authentic and approachable. Use a crisp, impactful cover image that reflects your field, your creative process, or your personal brand. This image should set the tone for your header and tell visitors at a glance what kind of professional you are, whether you are a developer, a designer, a researcher, or a business strategist.

Skills

List both your technical and soft skills, ranging from those you have mastered to those you are currently learning. Do not just list keywords; provide context. For each skill, explain a project or task where you successfully applied it in a real-world scenario. Explain the how and why. For example, if you list Python, do not just stop there. Describe how you used it to automate a specific task, analyze a dataset, or build a backend service. This helps others understand not just what you know, but how you think and solve problems.

Projects, Research & Case Studies

This is the most critical section for showcasing your expertise, regardless of your field. Think of this as your library of work. Whether you are in law, medicine, software engineering, design, or research, you can upload anything that demonstrates your skill: projects, published research, case studies, academic papers, or design iterations.

For technical work, include a direct link to a live demo, a GitHub repository, or documentation. Include screenshots of your code, architecture diagrams, or the live homepage. For research or legal or medical work, upload PDFs of your white papers, research findings, case analysis, or field reports. Explain the methodology you used and the significance of your findings.

For all fields, include a gallery of images or visuals for each entry. For every project or case study, provide a deep-dive summary: describe the problem you set out to solve, the approach you took, the tools or resources you utilized, and the ultimate outcome. By making your work tangible, you show others not just what you claim to do, but how you actually perform in your field.

Experience

Detail your professional history, including internships, freelance work, volunteer roles, and previous employment. For every position, focus on the substance of your contribution rather than just your job title. Use a paragraph to highlight the specific responsibilities you held, the obstacles you faced, and the tangible impact or results you achieved. If you helped a company increase efficiency, saved them time, or launched a successful product, explicitly mention that. This is the section where you prove that you can deliver results in a professional environment.

Certifications & Education

Clearly document your academic degrees, diplomas, and any professional certifications you have earned. Education is your foundation, so provide enough detail for someone to understand the scope of your studies. For each entry, provide a description detailing the core knowledge you gained. Explain how that specific degree or certification serves as the backbone for your current expertise. If you completed a specialized course, highlight the specific modules or final projects that demonstrate the depth of your understanding.

Insights & Articles

Document your insights, industry analysis, or regular field perspectives here. Writing your knowledge consistently demonstrates a deep understanding of your field and establishes you as a thoughtful practitioner. For each piece you add, focus on sharing a unique perspective or a lesson learned from your work. This section proves to potential employers and collaborators that you are not just executing tasks, but actively engaging with the theory, trends, and future of your industry.

Vision & Goals

This is your professional mission statement. Use this section to articulate where you are heading and the impact you aim to make in your field. Describe the long-term vision that drives your work and the specific, actionable goals you are setting for the coming months or years. By sharing your vision, you allow others, potential collaborators, mentors, or employers, to understand your underlying motivation and see if your aspirations align with their own projects or organizational goals.

Professional Titles

Add up to five professional titles that summarize your expertise. Clear, specific titles help recruiters, founders, and partners quickly understand your background and find you in the network. Think of these as the primary keywords that define your current professional offering; be as precise as possible about your role, such as Full-Stack Developer or Content Strategist, to ensure you appear in relevant searches.

What are you focusing on?

This is your space to document your ongoing journey and show the community your growth. Use this section to share what you are currently learning, the projects you are actively building, or the industry problems you are currently researching. Treat this as a living log—update it regularly to show that you are constantly moving forward. Sharing your focus gives others insight into your current trajectory and helps you connect with peers who are working on similar challenges.

Contact Methods & Social Links

This is your gateway for connection. Add your direct contact methods, such as your email, phone number, Telegram, or WhatsApp, and include links to your digital spaces like GitHub, LinkedIn, or personal websites. Providing multiple, accessible ways to reach you allows recruiters and potential partners to move quickly when they find a match. Keeping these updated ensures that the connection process is seamless and professional.
